MySpotsPlaces Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to MySpotsPlaces (the "Application"), a mobile application created by Murat Gazioğlu (the "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application may process information you choose to save in the app, such as place names, categories, notes, addresses, phone numbers, website links, favorite status, optional photos, and optional location coordinates for saved places.

If you allow location access, the Application may use your device location to show nearby places, sort places by distance, fill a saved place with your current location, and display your location on the map. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings.

The Application uses Apple iCloud/CloudKit to store and sync your saved places when iCloud is available on your device. Saved place information, including optional photos and optional location coordinates, may be stored in your private iCloud database so your data can remain available across your supported devices.

The Application uses Google Places services to provide nearby place discovery and place search. When you use these discovery or search features, related search text and location information may be sent to Google only as needed to return place results.

If you send feedback from inside the Application, the message you write, optional contact information, app version, iOS version, and selected app language may be sent to the Service Provider so the Service Provider can read your feedback, respond if needed, and improve the Application.

The Application does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not sell your personal information, and does not use your information for tracking across apps or websites.

Photos and User Content

Photos are accessed only when you choose a photo for a saved place. The selected photo may be stored locally on your device and, when iCloud sync is available, in your private iCloud database as part of that saved place.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved places remain in the Application until you edit or delete them. Removing the Application from your device may remove local app data from that device, but data synced through iCloud may remain available through your iCloud account unless removed from the Application or iCloud according to Apple's controls. Feedback messages may be retained as needed to review suggestions, provide support, and maintain records of communication.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop location access by changing the Application's permissions in your device settings. You can stop using the Application and remove local app data by uninstalling it using the standard uninstall process available on your device or through the mobile application marketplace.
